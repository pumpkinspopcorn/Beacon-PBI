Power BI offers a variety of visual types to represent data. For distribution analysis, Power BI provides bubble charts and grouped bar charts. In addition, it features part-to-whole visuals like donut charts, stacked bars, and treemaps, which help in displaying the division of components within a whole. Ranking visuals, such as ribbion charts, ordered columns, ordered bars, funnel charts, and decomposition trees, help to show relative positioning and performance across data points. Each visualization type is designed to address different analytical scenarios, such as relationship assessment, distribution, composition, and ranking.
